# Surface brief: portfolio home (index.html)

Mode: Experience. Visitor: potential client or church partner/peer who wants to see professional ability and how faith is carried out inside that profession. Not an interface for elders (confirmed by author 2026-10-13).
Action: read the work, open a project, contact by email. Proof: 14 real projects; no testimonials exist.
2026-11-23 redesign reasons (author): the ark world was too red and dark, the ark metaphor (甲板／艙室／肘) made the work hard to read, and the work was not visible at a glance. First viewport must be the work itself. Copy uses plain words, no ship metaphor.
Unresolved: `sermon-assist-core` inclusion; og-image.png and brand PNGs still show the retired red ark and need replacing by the author.

## Direction contract

OWN-WORLD: Neutral white (light) and near-black (dark, follows system) grounds, one palm-green accent for actions and focus, Geist plus Noto Sans TC, Geist Mono only for dates and counts.
